PhD and postdoc positions

  • PhD in Machine Learning in Medicine (applications received by 30 November 2026 receive full consideration)

Applications are accepted only via the University of Bern online portal (“Apply” button in the advertisement). Applications sent by email are not considered. Please read the FAQ below before contacting us: questions answered here will not receive an individual reply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Applying

How do I apply?

Applications are accepted only via the University of Bern online portal; please use the "Apply" button in the job advertisement. Applications, CVs or documents sent by email are not considered and are not forwarded to the portal.

I have already applied via the portal. Should I also email my CV or introduce myself?

No. All applications submitted through the portal are reviewed with the same care. Additional emails do not affect the evaluation.

The portal shows an error. What should I do?

Please try again later or with a different browser. If the problem persists, contact research.udem@insel.ch and include a screenshot of the error. Please do not send your application documents by email.

What is the deadline? Is the position still open?

Applications received by 30 November 2026 will receive full consideration. We may continue to review applications until the position is filled, and this page will be updated once it is.

Can you tell me whether my profile fits before I apply? Can we have a short call?

Due to the large number of enquiries, we are unable to assess individual profiles or arrange calls before the application deadline. If your background matches the requirements in the advertisement, we encourage you to apply. If you are excited about the position but don't meet every criterion, we still encourage you to apply.

When will I hear back?

Shortlisted candidates will be contacted for interviews after the application deadline. All other applicants will be contacted at the latest when the position has been filled.

Eligibility

My degree is not in computer science (e.g. statistics, physics, engineering, medicine). Can I apply?

Yes. We welcome applicants with a Master's degree in any quantitative discipline, provided you have solid knowledge of the foundations of machine learning and practical experience implementing and evaluating machine learning models in Python. A medical background is not required.

I have not completed my Master's degree yet. Can I apply?

Yes, if you will have completed your Master's degree before the start of the position. Please state your expected completion date in your CV. The start date can be agreed individually.

I already hold a PhD in another field. Can I apply?

This is possible as long as you are qualified for the position. The same requirements apply as for all applicants.

I have several years of industry experience. Is that a disadvantage?

No. Applicants with industry experience are welcome. The same requirements apply as for all applicants.

Can I apply from outside Europe?

Yes, we welcome applications from all countries. Candidates from outside the EU/EFTA require a Swiss work permit, which is applied for once an offer has been accepted. This typically takes a few months, which we take into account when agreeing on a start date.

What are the language requirements?

The working language of the lab is English. German is not required.

The position

Is the position funded?

Yes. This is a fully funded position with an employment contract at the University of Bern. The annual gross salary for 2026 is CHF 50,042 (full time, including the 13th month salary), according to SNSF rates. No external scholarship is needed.

How long is the position, and when does it start?

The contract runs for 3 years, with the possibility to extend by 1 year. The start date is as soon as possible or by agreement.

Can you support my scholarship application (e.g. Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship) or supervise a self-funded PhD?

We are currently not able to support scholarship applications or take on PhD students outside of advertised positions.

Do you have other PhD positions now or in the near future?

All open positions are announced on this page. We are not able to respond to general enquiries about future positions.

The project

Which data will the project use? Is it focused on a specific disease such as diabetes?

The project is not tied to a single disease. It will start from large, publicly available multimodal health cohorts combining imaging, wearable and clinical data.

Will there be treatment or intervention data for the causal modelling component?

The project will primarily use observational data, including treatment information as recorded in the cohorts (e.g. medication).

Can I bring in my own research interests, e.g. uncertainty quantification or interpretability?

Yes. The methodological focus areas listed in the advertisement are directions the candidate may explore, not a fixed plan, and there is room to shape the project within the overall topic.

Should I read something to prepare?

No specific preparation is needed to apply. If you are interested, our recent publications give a good impression of the lab's research.
