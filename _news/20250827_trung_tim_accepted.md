Two papers accepted for oral presentation at the MICCAI satellite events! Trung-Dung Hoang will present [A real-time digital twin for type 1 diabetes using simulation-based inference](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.01740) at the Digital Twin for Healthcare (DT4H) workshop, and Tim Flühmann will present [Label-free estimation of clinically relevant performance metrics under distribution shifts](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.22776) at the UNSURE workshop. Congratulations to the team!
