Two papers accepted at the MICCAI satellite events! Alceu Bissoto will present [On Evaluating Subgroup Discovery in Medical Image Classification](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026-sat/paper/FAIMI-BRIDGE-EPIMI_014.pdf) at the joint FAIMI, BRIDGE, and EPIMI workshop (oral presentation!), and Mareen Kalis will present [Quality over quantity: data curation for automated angle of progression estimation in intrapartum ultrasound](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026-sat/paper/PIPPI_004.pdf) at the PIPPI workshop. Special congratulations to Mareen for achieving this within the scope of her master thesis!
